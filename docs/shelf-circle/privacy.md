# Shelf Circle Privacy Policy

**Last updated: September 25, 2026**

Shelf Circle is a friends-only app for tracking what you read and sharing book recommendations
with people you know. This policy explains what information the Shelf Circle Android app
(package `com.shelfcircle.app`) and its servers collect, why, who can see it, and how you can
delete it. It applies to Shelf Circle only, not to other projects on this site.

Shelf Circle is made by **culpeo-labs** ("we", "us"). You can reach us at
**[CONTACT EMAIL — TO BE FILLED IN]**.

## The short version

- You sign in with your email. We store your email, a profile (handle, display name, optional
  photo), and the reading data you enter.
- Your friends — people you invited and who joined, or whom you approved — can see your name,
  handle, photo, and reading activity. They can see your full bookshelves only if you turn that
  on. Nobody else can look you up.
- We don't show ads, we don't sell your data, and we don't use analytics or advertising trackers.
- You can change or delete your information at any time. See
  [Your choices and deleting your data](#your-choices-and-deleting-your-data).

## Information we collect

### Information you give us

| What | Why we need it |
| --- | --- |
| **Email address**, and the sign-in credentials you choose (a one-time code, a password, or a passkey, depending on how sign-in is configured) | To create your account and sign you in. Credentials are handled by our sign-in provider, Hanko (see [Service providers](#service-providers)); **we never see or store your password.** We receive and store your email address. |
| **Profile**: handle (username), display name, and an optional profile photo | To identify you to your friends. |
| **Reading data**: the books you add, the shelf each is on (want to read, reading, finished, did not finish), reading progress, star ratings, when you finish a book, and whether you logged it as something you read before using the app | To run the app: your shelves, your timeline, and your yearly reading count. |
| **Reading goals** you set (for example, "24 books in 2026") | To show your progress. |
| **Friends, invites, and recommendations**: who you are friends with, invite links you create, and recommendations you send or receive, including any note you write (up to 280 characters) | To connect you with friends and deliver recommendations. |
| **Your library**: which public library system you chose (for example, Seattle Public Library) | To link book pages to that library's catalog. |
| **Settings**: whether you share your bookshelves with friends | To respect your choice. |

### Information from your device

- **Time zone.** The app sends your device's time zone so your yearly reading count changes
  over on New Year's Day where you live. We store it alongside a reading goal if you set one.
- **Camera (only if you scan an invite).** The app asks for camera permission to scan a friend's
  invite QR code. Camera images are processed on your device to read the code; **they are not
  stored or uploaded.** You can decline, and open the invite link instead.
- **Photos (only if you choose a profile picture).** The app uses Android's photo picker, so it
  can access only the single image you select. The image is cropped and resized on your device,
  then uploaded as your profile photo.
- **Sign-in session.** Your sign-in token is kept in your device's secure storage so you stay
  signed in. The app also remembers, on your device, when you last looked at your recommendations
  (for the unread badge).

### Technical information

Like most online services, our servers and hosting provider process technical information needed
to deliver and protect the service, such as your IP address, the time of a request, and error
details. Application logs are kept for up to 30 days.

### What we don't collect

We do not collect your location, contacts, advertising ID, or browsing activity. The app has no
advertising and no third-party analytics or crash-reporting tools.

## How we use your information

We use your information only to provide and secure Shelf Circle: to sign you in, show your
shelves and timeline, connect you with friends, deliver recommendations, count your yearly
reading, look up books and library links, fix problems, and prevent abuse. We don't use it for
advertising or to build marketing profiles, and we don't sell it.

## Who can see your information

- **Your friends.** Friends are added only through an invite link or QR code that you create
  (see below). Your friends can see your display name, handle, and photo, and your reading
  activity in their timeline (for example, that you started or finished a book) — except books
  you log as read *before* you started using Shelf Circle, which are kept out of the timeline.
  Friends also see the recommendations you send them, including your note. You can only send
  recommendations to your friends.
- **Your bookshelves, if you choose.** By default only you can see your shelves. If you turn on
  **Share my bookshelves with friends** (Me tab), your friends can see what you are reading, have
  read, and want to read, with your ratings and progress. You can turn this off at any time.
- **Your reading goal and yearly count** are visible only to you.
- **People who aren't your friends** can't search for you or look up your profile. Your
  **email address and account ID are never shown to other users**, and your handle is shown only
  to your friends.
- **Invite links.** You choose how each invite works:
  - *One person:* a link or QR code that works once and expires after 7 days. Whoever uses it
    first becomes your friend straight away, so share it only with the person you mean.
  - *Anyone with the link:* a link that works for up to 30 days, or until you stop it, for
    sharing more widely. Each person who uses it sends you a request that shows only their
    display name and photo, and **they become your friend only if you approve.**

  Anyone who has an invite link before it is used up or expires can see your display name and
  photo (and whether approval is needed), so treat it like a private link.
- **Your profile photo** is stored at a web address that isn't listed anywhere but that anyone
  who has the address can open. If you replace or remove your photo, we don't automatically
  delete the old file, so it can remain reachable at its old address; email us if you want an old
  photo removed. Deleting your account removes your photos.

## Service providers

We use a small number of companies to run Shelf Circle. They handle information only to provide
their service to us, not for their own purposes.

- **Hanko** ([hanko.io](https://www.hanko.io/privacy)) provides sign-in. It processes your email
  and sign-in credentials and issues the token that proves who you are.
- **Microsoft Azure** hosts our servers, our database, and profile-photo storage. Our data is
  hosted in the United States (Azure West US). Azure encrypts stored data at rest.
- **Book and library lookups.** When you search for a book, your search text is sent from our
  servers to **Open Library** (Internet Archive) and, when enabled, **Google Books**. When you
  open "Get it at your library", the book's title, author, and ISBN are sent from our servers to
  your library's online catalog (currently BiblioCommons-hosted catalogs) to find the book. These
  requests come from our servers and **don't include your name, email, or account.** Tapping
  the resulting link opens the library's own website in your browser, which is covered by that
  library's privacy policy.
- **Google Play** distributes the app and collects its own information under
  [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect the safety of our users
or our service.

## How long we keep your information

We keep your information while your account exists. When you delete your account we delete your
profile and reading data from our database, and your profile photo from storage. Encrypted
database backups are kept for up to 7 days and then expire, and application logs for up to 30
days, so residual copies can persist for that long after deletion.

## Your choices and deleting your data

- **Edit or remove your information.** In the app you can change your display name and photo,
  remove your photo, change or remove your reading goal, and choose whether friends see your
  bookshelves.
- **Delete your account and data.** Email us at **[CONTACT EMAIL — TO BE FILLED IN]** from the
  address on your account, with the subject "Delete my Shelf Circle account". We'll delete your
  account and all data described above, including your sign-in record with Hanko, within 30
  days and confirm by email.
- **Access or correct your information.** You can see most of it in the app. If you'd like a
  copy of the rest, or something corrected, email us.

If you live somewhere that gives you rights over your personal information (for example, the
EU/UK, or California), you can use the same email address to exercise them. We don't sell or
"share" personal information for cross-context advertising.

## Security

Everything between the app and our servers travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Passwords
are handled by our sign-in provider and never reach our servers. Your sign-in token is kept in
your device's secure storage. No system is perfectly secure, so we can't promise absolute
security, but we work to protect your information and will tell affected users if a breach puts
their information at risk.

## Children

Shelf Circle is not directed to children under 13, and you must be at least 13 to use it. We
don't knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us
information, email us and we'll delete it.

## International users

Our servers are in the United States. If you use Shelf Circle from elsewhere, your information
is transferred to and processed in the United States.
